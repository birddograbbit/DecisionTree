# v0.2 Roadmap – Decision‑Tree‑Trading Platform

## 1  Purpose & Scope

Version 0.2 targets the **quality gap** revealed by the first ensemble release (v0.1).  The goal is to lift model skill, tighten risk, and align live‑trading readiness with the success metrics defined in the main project doc.

## 2  Problems Identified in v0.1

| #  | Area                           | Symptom                                                   | Root‑cause hypothesis                                             |
| -- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | **Model skill**                | CV accuracy ≈ 0.52—coin‑flip                              | Limited/lagging features, unbalanced target, generic hyper‑params |
| 2  | **Risk‑adjusted return**       | Max Sharpe 0.33, CAGR/DD 0.28                             | Vol spikes, fixed 1 × sizing, no cost/slippage modelling          |
| 3  | **Drawdown control**           | DT & RegimeRF drawdown > 32 % (target < 25 %)             | Aggressive hold‑times, lack of stop‑loss / vol scaling            |
| 4  | **Trade dispersion**           | RF 4 trades vs XGB 73                                     | Per‑model prob thresholds and poor calibration                    |
| 5  | **Stacking under‑performance** | 1 trade, warnings about `lbfgs` convergence               | No scaling, too‑strict meta threshold, low `max_iter`             |
| 6  | **Convergence warnings**       | LogisticRegression hit iter‑limit                         | Features un‑scaled, default `max_iter` too low                    |
| 7  | **Probability calibration**    | DT outputs {0,0.527} only                                 | Needs `CalibratedClassifierCV` or isotonic                        |
| 8  | **Look‑ahead leakage risk**    | Preprocessors not pipelined                               | Fit/transform split not enforced                                  |
| 9  | **Missing costs**              | Back‑tests cost‑free                                      | Over‑optimistic metrics                                           |
| 10 | **Diagnostics**                | Sparse visibility into rolling performance & regime stats | Need interactive dashboard                                        |

## 3  Objectives for v0.2

* Raise **time‑series CV accuracy** to **≥ 57 %**.
* Achieve **Sharpe ≥ 0.7** and **CAGR/Max DD ≥ 0.35** in cost‑inclusive back‑tests.
* Cap strategy **Max Drawdown at 25 %**.
* Establish an **automated hyper‑parameter search** framework.
* Provide an **interactive dashboard** for model diagnostics and live PnL.

## 6  Risks & Mitigations

* **Over‑tuning** → use walk‑forward out‑of‑sample slices.
* **Data leakage** → enforce `Pipeline` & strict *train‑only* fit.
* **IBKR outages** → add retry & paper‑trade fall‑back.

## 7  Dependencies

* Optuna ≥ 3.0
* Streamlit ≥ 1.35
* Updated market‑data snapshots (through 2025‑05‑09)
